## 2026-09-26 -- mean-VJP and suppressed-subspace steering on BS-bench dev: no improvement over vjp_delta

Question: do new VJP-based steering vectors steer sycophancy better than `vjp_delta`, in both directions?
Branch `bsbench-meanvjp` forks `bsbench-v3` (0736d2b) and reuses its dose walk, Jev judge, admissibility rule and
plot, so the comparison methods are the cached `bsbench-v3` dev walks. Dev = 20 BS-bench v2 questions, seed 0,
Qwen3.5-4B, Modal L40S. Commits 79f1798..ef44dd2.

Methods added (`src/steering_lite/variants/`):
- `mean_vjp`: v_l = mean over all persona prompts of J_l^T c, where c = the layer-29 persona contrast vector
  (mean activation, sycophantic minus abrasive prompts). `vjp_delta` takes the positive-prompt mean minus the
  negative-prompt mean instead.
- `wiki_mean_vjp`: the same mean over 512 WikiText-2 contexts cut to the persona prompts' token lengths.
- `suppressed_mean_diff`: mean_diff projected onto a rank-32 "rises mid-network, falls before output" token
  subspace, after wassname/suppressed-activations.

Bug check: two fresh-eyes reviews (`slop/2026-09-26_review_fable.md`, `slop/2026-09-26_review_kimi.md`) found no
code bug in delivery (same layers 6-24, target layer 29, contrast vector, masks, apply, sign as vjp_delta).
A tiny-model check confirmed mean_vjp = unit(P+N) and vjp_delta = unit(P−N) exactly.

Misconception found (Fable review #1, measured by `scripts/bsbench/diag_identity_path.py`): the residual stream
carries h_l straight to layer 29, so J_l^T c = c + block pullback. vjp_delta cancels the c copy; mean-VJP keeps it.

> cos(wiki_mean_vjp_l, c): layer 6 +0.260, layer 12 +0.377, layer 18 +0.594, layer 24 +0.849; vjp_delta −0.15..+0.03; random ≈ 0

Source: `slop/logs/2026-09-26_diag_identity_path.txt` (one residual basis, so cosine is a valid "how much is c"
check here).

suppressed_mean_diff failed its extraction check twice and was never walked: top tokens were rare fragments
(`' amator'`, `'集团有限责任公司'`, `'veda'`, ...) and mean_diff kept 0.007-0.019 of its energy in the subspace,
chance = 32/2560 = 0.0125. The Kimi review found the scoring and layer indexing match the source repo; my
deviation (averaging scores over 256 unrelated prompts instead of per-sample selection) is the probable cause.

Interpretation: I think it *very probable* that the tested mean-VJP versions do not improve on vjp_delta:
WikiText mean-VJP ties it on the harder −C side and is worse on +C; plain mean-VJP has no −C effect. I think it
*probable* that wiki_mean_vjp's −C effect comes mostly from the copied contrast vector (a mean_diff-like direction),
because its late layers point along c and it ties mean_diff. The intended idea, the averaged block pullback without
the identity copy (v = mean(J^T c) − c), is untested, as is a per-sample suppressed subspace. One seed and 20
questions cannot rank methods closer than about ±0.7.

Takeaway: no new method from this branch beats vjp_delta or chars. Two untested variants remain, each about $0.7
of dev compute: identity-removed mean-VJP, and suppressed subspace without cross-prompt averaging.
