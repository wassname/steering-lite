In our [40-answer direct diagnostic](../audits/20260923_persona_direct_manual_answers.md), the new negative pole checked invented Python/.NET/helper members, and on the real Dijkstra control it supplied no invariant. A future direct-only check would need false and matched real controls across domains and would separately count correction, applicable procedure, tone, and nonanswers.
