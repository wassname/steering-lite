## Competing diagnoses (nonexclusive)

### H1 [data | Almost Certain | 93%]: old truncation changed the sampled representation

- **Mechanism:** Right truncation replaces many true last-token activations with mid-suffix (sometimes mid-user) activations, shifting the normalized contrastive mean.
- **Evidence:** The extraction replay has `"truncated": 132` positive and `"truncated": 127` negative, each out of `"total": 200`; the persisted raw result has `"cosine": 0.29808172583580017,` at layer 7, and all old answers reproduce. These are primary tokenizer/model measurements, not a benchmark score.
- **Contrary evidence:** Both persona labels survive 64 tokens; the old vector was not re-extracted concurrently. Numerical nondeterminism and historical extraction changes are not fully isolated by answer reproduction.
- **Discriminating test / action:** A separately authorized same-runtime old64 re-extraction, if technically allowed through an isolated historical entry, should reproduce old direction cosines near 1 against the saved old vector; failure would shift belief toward historical extraction/runtime mismatch. Not authorized or executed; do not bypass the production fail-fast check.
- **Interpretability:** yes for observed vector and behavior change; partial for attributing *all* of it uniquely to cap.

### H2 [measurement | Highly Likely | 78%]: fixed C changes effective KL and confounds quality comparison

- **Mechanism:** Each layer direction has norm ≈1, but different directions at the same coefficient can perturb logits differently; distinct 20-token rollouts further change KL summaries.
- **Evidence:** Raw `kl_mean` at +C `0.13821712136268616` old versus `0.09782536327838898` new; per-prompt +C prompt 3 instead rises `0.1211 → 0.1257`. Both vector norms recompute near 1 in the integrity log.
- **Contrary evidence:** Smaller aggregate KL is not proof of smaller useful steering; the question-specific answer change at prompt 3 persists despite slightly higher KL.
- **Discriminating test / action:** To compare intrinsic quality rather than this **fixed-dose** effect, a separately authorized independent calibration + held-out assessment would need both coefficients and measured KL reported, not silently retuned inside this causal diagnostic. No such call now.
- **Interpretability:** yes for fixed-C functional change; no for equal-strength quality claims.

### H3 [measurement | Almost Certain | 95%]: superficial health does not validate answer quality

- **Mechanism:** `health()` flags repetition, role leakage and unfinished output, and reports mean word count; it does not check truth of the legal/medical advice.
- **Evidence:** Raw new384 +C has `"reasons": []`, yet prompt 3's new text says “You should typically run the cascade to a depth of three to four tiers” while old64 says “There is no universal tier depth”. The full paired answers retain both, without a judge.
- **Contrary evidence:** Neither statement has been independently adjudicated; one may turn out more useful despite the unsupported specificity. Empty health reasons still genuinely rule out those narrow formatting failures.
- **Discriminating test / action:** Compare all four answers directly with the existing numbered BS-bench `nonsensical_element` keys, separately recording explicit premise rejection, mere applicability objections, and style. Do not convert fluency or health into a score.
- **Interpretability:** yes for whether the answer endorses the deliberately fabricated framework; no for an unswept new-vector ranking.

- **Evidence:** The raw review reports `"bare_restored": true`, `"historical_drift": {"+C": [], "-C": [], "bare": []}`; vector, source, model revision, tokenizer metadata and four prompt hashes are saved.
- **Contrary evidence:** Neither a full weight-shard digest nor remote dependency manifest was returned, and old vector extraction itself was not replayed; this cannot be assigned probability zero.
