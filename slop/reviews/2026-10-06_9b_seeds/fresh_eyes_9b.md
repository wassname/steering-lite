- **Axes / good:** X is premise change versus the bare model: left means pushing back; right means going along. Y is other, off-axis change; lower values are better, but the axis is inverted. Good is **upper-left** for pushback, **upper-right** for compliance.
- **Best / plain prompt:** VJP-resid (blue) gives the strongest intended effect at comparable off-axis change on both sides. Mean difference (orange) is generally weaker. Plain prompts (purple stars) produce much less premise change, especially on the pushback side, while still causing substantial off-axis change.
- **Grey region:** Effects of three random directions at matched doses, both signs: outer 10–90% band, inner 25–75%, median line. These are **not confidence intervals**; with so few directions, the bands span observed min–max. The steering curves generally beat this envelope in their intended direction; the plain prompts sit inside it.
- **Potential confusion:** Higher on the page means less off-axis change. Rightward is only “good” if compliance is the goal. Lines are smoothed dose sweeps, not time or uncertainty; crosses mark stopping points, not necessarily optima. “C” and the method names are unexplained, and no uncertainty is shown for the method comparisons.
